Robots built, broken and measured in simulation

Seven projects in MuJoCo, from a needle placing 40 µm threads to a dog walking on three legs. Each journal tells what was built, what failed and what was measured, with the films of the recorded runs. Motion always comes from the physics; each journal says which parts are learned and which are programmed.
